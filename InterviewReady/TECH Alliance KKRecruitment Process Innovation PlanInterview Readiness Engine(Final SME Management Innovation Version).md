1. Executive SummaryTECH Alliance KK is a Tokyo-based recruitment and staffing company specializing in IT and engineering talent acquisition. Established approximately three years ago, the company has achieved stable growth and currently generates approximately JPY 100 million in annual revenue through recruitment and staffing services supporting both Japanese enterprises and multinational corporations operating in Japan.As Japan faces increasing labor shortages, globalization of the workforce, and rising complexity in IT hiring, TECH Alliance KK plans to implement a Recruitment Process Innovation initiative centered on the “Interview Readiness Engine,” a practical AI-assisted recruitment workflow improvement framework specifically designed for the Japanese market.This initiative is not intended to become a generic large-scale AI platform business. Instead, it is positioned as a realistic operational innovation project focused on:• Productivity Improvement (生産性向上)• Reduction of consultant-dependent operations (属人化解消)• Recruitment workflow standardization (業務標準化)• Digital Transformation (DX推進)• Added value enhancement (付加価値向上)By integrating AI into selected recruitment workflows, TECH Alliance KK aims to transform accumulated recruitment know-how into a reusable and scalable operational framework while maintaining the company’s human-centered consulting strengths.The project is expected to:• Improve interview pass rates by 15–25%• Increase consultant productivity by 30–40%• Reduce interview preparation time per candidate by 20–30%• Improve operational scalability without proportional headcount growth• Strengthen support for Tokyo SMEs and international enterprises facing IT talent shortagesThis initiative aligns strongly with Japan’s SME Management Innovation objectives 
and contributes directly to improving operational efficiency and international talent utilization within Tokyo’s labor market.

Recruitment Industry Challenges in JapanJapan continues to face structural labor shortages, particularly in IT and engineering sectors. At the same time, recruitment activities are becoming increasingly internationalized.Major operational challenges include:• Differences between Japanese-style and global interview expectations• Increasing workload for interview preparation support• Repetitive and consultant-dependent manual coaching operations• Lack of standardized interview preparation processes• Difficulty for foreign candidates to understand implicit Japanese hiring expectations• Difficulty for Japanese candidates to adapt to global/MNC interview styles• Longer hiring cycles and rising recruitment costs for employersThese challenges reduce recruitment productivity and create operational bottlenecks for recruitment firms and HR organizations.
4. Recruitment Process Innovation – Interview Readiness Engine4.1 Project ConceptThe Interview Readiness Engine is an AI-assisted recruitment process improvement framework designed to improve candidate preparation quality, operational productivity, and recruitment efficiency.Rather than replacing consultants, the project standardizes repetitive interview preparation processes and enables consultants to focus on higher-value consulting activities.Key operational objectives include:• Productivity Improvement (生産性向上)• Reduction of consultant-dependent operations (属人化解消)• Workflow Standardization (業務標準化)• Digital Transformation of recruitment operations (DX推進)• Improvement of operational added value (付加価値向上)4.2 Target UsersPhase 1:• Internal consultants of TECH Alliance KK• Selected candidates represented by TECH Alliance KKPhase 2:• Most candidates represented by TECH Alliance KK• IT and engineering job seekers in JapanFuture Expansion:• Corporate HR support functions for SMEs and multinational corporations
5. Core Functional Scope (Phase 1 MVP)To ensure practical implementation feasibility and operational effectiveness, Phase 1 focuses only on core productivity improvement modules.5.1 JD Insight – Job Description Analysis• AI-assisted analysis of job descriptions• Identification of required competencies and implicit expectations• Detection of interview focus areas and preparation priorities• Gap analysis between candidate experience and job requirements5.2 Resume Alignment Support• Resume optimization guidance based on job descriptions• Improvement recommendations for Japanese and English resumes• Highlighting of relevant project and achievement experience• Support for adapting resumes to Japanese and global hiring styles5.3 AI Mock Interview (Text-Based)• Simulated interview questions based on job descriptions• Japanese, English, and bilingual interview preparation• Behavioral and technical interview practice• Structured feedback generation5.4 Japanese Interview Risk Point Analysis• Identification of common interview mistakes• Analysis of communication risks and evaluation concerns• Guidance on Japanese interview expectations and communication style• Detection of excessive humility, over-assertion, unclear logic, and cultural mismatch risksTo minimize development risk, the initial implementation intentionally avoids large-scale AI avatar systems, enterprise SaaS infrastructure, and high-cost video technologies.
